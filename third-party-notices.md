TOML uses pinned go-toml 2.4.3 with strict decoding; the settings editor uses that same version's byte-range AST to preserve handwritten comments. Unknown-field, inheritance and permission behavior belongs to smolpng. Huh 2.0.3/Bubble Tea 2.0.2 supply keyboard/forms and terminal lifecycle rather than a homemade terminal driver. Their transitive width/color/key libraries are retained above. The atotto clipboard dependency is used by text-field paste behavior, not smolpng's smart multi-format output. `golang.design/x/clipboard` 0.9.0 provides native PNG/files/text representations through a single write; its actual graphical behavior must be qualified per host. Go's term package detects genuine terminal sessions.

The licenses in this table permit the selected use with their notices retained. This is not a claim of a live security-advisory audit, universal native compatibility or legal qualification of a differently bundled package. Only the selected exact cached source was compiled; no unverified latest version was substituted.

pngquant keeps its upstream GPL/commercial licensing and additional notices: https://pngquant.org/licensing.html . OxiPNG is MIT: https://github.com/oxipng/oxipng . Neither executable is bundled in standard smolpng archives. Managed OxiPNG 10.2.1 downloads use upstream release-metadata SHA-256 pins and retain its exact MIT notice in the separate installation. PNGquant has no automatic download in this build. Containers and Store packaging have separate composition checks; merely launching a separate process is not universal legal clearance.

No font files, commissioned artwork, theme imagery, private profiles, credentials or toolchain caches are included. Header art is a replaceable project-written text placeholder. The selected MIT, CC0 and AGPL texts under `LICENSES/` do not apply a blanket AGPL license to the application.
